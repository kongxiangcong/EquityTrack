# Kimi Datasource live probes

The live probe payloads are local research material, not production cache,
official evidence, reusable Provider fixtures, or repository-distributable
data. They are intentionally excluded from Git.

The retained conclusion is:

- a successful transport or process exit does not prove usable business data;
- output extensions do not prove the payload format;
- Kimi results lack sufficient upstream authority and PIT metadata for the
  production Provider role;
- announcements discovered through auxiliary sources are candidates for
  official-source resolution, not official Evidence;
- request IDs and tool-call IDs are diagnostics, not provenance.

The governing findings and validation rules are in
[Kimi Datasource 作为数据 Provider 的可行性研究](../kimi-datasources-provider.md).
Connection and credential boundaries are in [tushare_usage.md](tushare_usage.md).
